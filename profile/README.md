# Karolusia

**A European commons for artificial intelligence.** · [karolusia.com](https://karolusia.com)

A language model that nobody owns. It is trained and served by the computers of the people who use it: no supercomputer, no investors, no dependence on any tech giant.

## Our promises

- **Owned by no one, so it belongs to everyone.** No vendor, no host and no shareholder decides on behalf of the community.
- **Total transparency.** Everything the app does with your machine, your data and your electricity is stated plainly, measured and displayed. The model's limits, incidents and evaluation results are published.
- **No ads, no tokens, no hidden incentives. Ever.** No interface that nudges you into contributing without understanding.
- **Your machine stays yours.** The app only contributes while your device is idle. It stops as soon as you take it back.
- **Your data stays with you.** Nothing personal leaves your device without your explicit consent.
- **European all the way.** Trained from scratch on Europe's open written heritage, in line with the GDPR, the AI Act and copyright law, with documented data provenance.
- **Open code.** The software is released under the European licence EUPL 1.2, and independent audits check that the distributed app matches the code.

## The first product: Karolus learns in public

You don't install Karolusia for a finished model, but to watch the European model grow. The app shows training progress, network counters and the evaluation of every version. You chat with the current Karolus, which improves week after week in front of the people who help it grow.

The more of us there are, the more it knows.

## Join the Ost

The name comes from Charlemagne (*Karolus* in Latin), who brought Europe together and put the written word back at its centre. The Ost, named after the army a medieval king raised, is the network of contributors: everyone lends their computer when they are not using it, and gets access to the model in return.
